# Business case — synthetic invoice review
## Buyer and problem
Financial-operations leaders need faster invoice review without sacrificing policy compliance or auditability. Today invoice checks may require manual policy lookup, vendor verification and approval routing. This is a representative hypothesis, not validated customer discovery.

## Value hypothesis
Reduce analyst preparation time and missing-evidence rates while maintaining zero unauthorized disclosures and zero unauthorized approvals in the test suite.

## Measurement
Baseline manual task time, assisted task time, correction rate, citation validity, policy violations, approval cycle time, per-case inference cost. Report sample size and uncertainty; no invented savings.

## Non-goals
Real payments, customer PII, compliance certification, multi-cloud enterprise deployment, broad procurement automation.
