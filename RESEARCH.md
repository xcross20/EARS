# Research register
## Hypotheses to test
H1: invoice preparation time falls relative to a manual baseline without increased policy violations.
H2: pre-retrieval access filtering prevents cross-tenant and unauthorized-document exposure.
H3: bounded engineering loops reduce regressions versus unreviewed AI-generated code.

## Evidence discipline
Separate observations, assumptions, external references and conclusions. Record sample sizes, counterexamples, limitations, and evaluation data provenance. Never present synthetic benchmark results as measured enterprise ROI.
